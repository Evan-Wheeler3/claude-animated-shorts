# The Retired Couple Who Cracked The Lottery: script v1 (person beats a system, for the script gate)

Narrator voice B. ~183 words (~58 s; traffic-lights ran 3.1 words/s). Experiment (LEARNING-LOG.md): **visual simplicity** (ANIMATION-PLAYBOOK.md 1b):
one subject per shot, plain bright sets, a new picture every 2–2.5 s. Story shape from the two reference Shorts:
person + stunt in line 1 → why it works → a setback → the fix → trouble → a huge number → a closing image.
Title options: "He Cracked The Lottery In 2 Minutes" · "The Retired Couple Who Beat The Lottery" · "The Lottery Had A Math Mistake"

L01 A retired shop owner cracked the lottery in under two minutes.
L02 He was reading a brochure for a game called WinFall.
L03 If nobody won the jackpot, the money rolled down to people who matched just a few numbers.
L04 Jerry did the math. On those weeks, if you bought enough tickets, you'd win back more than you spent.
L05 So he bet twenty-two hundred dollars... and lost fifty.
L06 Not enough tickets. So he bet eight thousand, and won almost sixteen thousand.
L07 His wife Marge was in. They started a company, sold shares to friends and family, and bought tickets by the hundreds of thousands.
L08 When Michigan dropped the game, they drove fourteen hours to Massachusetts, which had the same one.
L09 Then a newspaper noticed a few stores selling millions in tickets, and the state suspected organized crime.
L10 Investigators found a retired couple from Michigan... doing math. And it was all legal.
L11 By the end, their group had won nearly twenty-seven million dollars, almost eight million of it profit.
L12 *(pause, ~0.4 s)* And they kept every single ticket in tubs in their barn, just in case of an audit.
