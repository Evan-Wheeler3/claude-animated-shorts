# lottery-couple: shot list (script v2, approved 2026-11-28)

Built to **ANIMATION-PLAYBOOK.md 1b** (the experiment): one subject per picture, nameable in two words; plain bright sets
(flat-colour walls, clean floor, empty sky); characters big (a third to half the frame height); a new picture every
~2–2.5 s; the picture becomes the named thing within ~0.3 s of its word. Wide shots ≤ 2 s.
Beats are timed with `T(line, word)` after the voice is aligned. **27 pictures in ~58 s ≈ 2.1 s each.**

Cast (toon cards, `refStyle()`): **Jerry** (60s, glasses, grey hair, cardigan), **Marge** (60s, short grey curls, cardigan),
**cashier**, **reporter**, **investigator** (trench coat, hat), 3–4 **friends** (colour variants).
Sets (all built in code, flat colours): kitchen (yellow wall, table), shop counter (mint wall), plain "tabletop" for props
(warm beige), road (sky + green + grey road), newspaper office desk, barn (red wall, open doors).
Library: `kenney-car-kit/sedan` (the road), `kenney-car-kit/box` / `medieval-village-megakit/prop-crate` if needed. Everything
else is simple code geometry: tickets, cash stacks, coins, calculator, jackpot pile, stairs, newspaper, magnifier, tubs.
No Codex brief needed (nothing bespoke the library lacks that isn't a box with a label).
