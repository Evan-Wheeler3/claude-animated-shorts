# Why Matches Only Light On The Box: script v1 (Fix-Fail Chain, for the script gate)

Narrator voice B. ~175 words (~55-60 s at voice B's 2.9 words/s). Second person, no dates or names. Facts: STORY.md.
Title options: "Why Matches Only Light On The Box" · "Why Safety Matches Are Safe" · "The Match That Rotted Jaws"

L01 To make fire, you rub two sticks together. But it takes forever, and your arms give out.
L02 So you dip a chemical stick into acid, and it bursts into flame. But now you carry a bottle of acid.
L03 So you make a match you drag through sandpaper. But it stinks of sulfur, and burning bits fall on the carpet.
L04 So you swap in white phosphorus, and it lights on anything. But it lights so easily, it has to live in a metal tin.
L05 And its fumes rot the jaws of the workers who make it. Their jawbones even glow in the dark.
L06 So you split the fire in two. One chemical goes on the head, and red phosphorus goes on the side of the box.
L07 Apart, neither half can light. Strike them together, and they flare. That is why it is a safety match!
L08 Later, countries banned white phosphorus matches for good.
L09 So every match you strike works like the very first fire. Rubbing two things together. Just the right two!
