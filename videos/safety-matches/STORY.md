# Why matches only light on the box: fact sheet (Wikipedia pages read 2026-11-26; quotes verbatim unless marked)

| # | Fact | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Fire by friction (rubbing sticks) is slow, hard work | general knowledge, not narrated as a claim with numbers | Safe (no numbers) |
| F2 | Chancel's 1805 match: chemical head "ignited by dipping its tip in a small asbestos bottle filled with sulfuric acid"; "quite expensive... relatively dangerous" | [Match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Match) | Safe |
| F3 | Walker's friction match (1826/27): drawn through folded sandpaper ("glass-paper") to light; "The sulfur's unpleasant odor" (camphor added) | [John Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Walker_(inventor)), Match | Safe |
| F4 | Early friction matches: "lit fragments sometimes falling to the floor and burning carpets and dresses" (bans in France and Germany) | Match | Safe (said of early friction matches generally, not Walker's specifically) |
| F5 | White phosphorus match (Sauria, 1830/31) "could light when struck against any surface... needed to be stored in airtight metal containers to avoid accidental ignition" | Match | Safe |
| F6 | Phossy jaw: "caused by white phosphorus vapor, which destroys the bones of the jaw"; "Affected bones glowed a greenish-white colour in the dark" | [Phossy jaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phossy_jaw) | Safe |
| F7 | Red phosphorus (1840s) "more stable"; "a striking surface on the box that contained red phosphorus"; safety match credited to Gustaf Erik Pasch, 1850s-60s | [Matchgirls' strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matchgirls%27_strike), John Walker | Safe ("red phosphorus on the side of the box") |
| F8 | Safety matches "can be struck only against a specially prepared surface" | Match (intro) | Safe |
| F9 | Berne Convention (1906, in force 1912) prohibits white phosphorus in matches; national bans from 1872 (Finland) | [Berne Convention (1906)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berne_Convention_(1906)), Phossy jaw | Safe ("countries banned") |
| F10 | Friction converting red to white phosphorus at the strike | not found in sources read | **Avoid** |
| F11 | Matches lighting "in your pocket" | only in a summary, not verbatim | **Avoid**; say "sealed in a metal tin" |
