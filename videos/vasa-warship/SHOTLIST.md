## Continuity
- Screen direction: the ship sails **right to left** throughout, so exterior shots see its **port** side. It heels **to port** (fact), so the masts tip **toward the camera**, which reads clearly on a phone. Keep the camera on the port side for every exterior heel/sinking shot; shots from the bow or stern show the roll as a screen tilt.
- The same 4 sails set in pics 15-21; the lower gunports **open** in pics 16-21, closed in pics 7-13.
- The officer's design is consistent in pics 7-13. The King appears only in pic 6.
- Water level and the Stockholm skyline match between pics 14, 19, 21 and 27.
