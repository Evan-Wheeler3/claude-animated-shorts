# Neighbor dialogue

Record one dry track in the same calm host voice throughout. Apply device coloration in the mix. In and out points below are edit targets at 30 fps. Out points are exclusive.

| Cue | In–out frames | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | 120–180 | Welcome back. Episode forty-six. |
| H02 | 450–507 | Tonight’s guest just learned to stand. |
| H03 | 810–870 | So how’d you get into this? |
| H04 | 1200–1230 | Welcome back. |
| H05 | 1320–1470 | So when did you know it was time to move out of your parents’ cave? |

H01 plays from the porch feed, H02 through the nursery monitor, H03 from the camera inside the safe, and H04 and H05 from the trail cam. Leave the bear’s breath to the effects track.
