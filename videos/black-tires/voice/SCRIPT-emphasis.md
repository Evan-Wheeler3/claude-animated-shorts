L01 The first car tires were not black. They were *white*!
L02 Rubber is naturally creamy white, and makers added a white powder for *grip*.
L03 But white tires wore out *fast*.
L04 So tire makers mixed in carbon black, a fine black powder that is basically *soot*.
L05 It makes the rubber *tougher* and pulls heat out of the tread, so tires last *far* longer.
L06 Today, a tire can be up to *forty* percent carbon black.
L07 So tires are not black for style. They are black because *soot* makes them *last*!
