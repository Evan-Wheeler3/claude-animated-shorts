In 1945, a German submarine was brought down by its own toilet.

U-1206 was hiding off Scotland, two hundred feet underwater.

Its new high-pressure toilet was so complicated, flushing it could require a specialist.

One day, the flushing system went wrong.

Instead of going out, seawater came blasting in.

The water spilled into the battery room below, releasing poisonous chlorine gas.

The crew had to surface—and Allied aircraft could easily spot the surfaced submarine.

The submarine was damaged and abandoned. Eventually sinking to the ocean floor.

All because of a bathroom break.

This was the worst flush in naval history.
