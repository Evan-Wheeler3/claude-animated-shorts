In 1945, a German submarine was brought down by its own toilet.

U-1206 was hiding off Scotland, two hundred feet underwater.

Its new high-pressure toilet was so complicated, flushing it could require a specialist.

Then the flushing system went wrong.

Instead of going out, seawater came blasting in.

It reached the batteries below, releasing poisonous chlorine gas.

The crew had to surface—right where Allied aircraft could spot them.

The submarine was damaged, abandoned, and scuttled.

All because of a bathroom break.

Worst flush in naval history.
