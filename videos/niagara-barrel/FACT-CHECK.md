# Niagara Barrel: fact check (Gate 2, 2026-11-17)

| Claim in SCRIPT.md | Finding | Confidence | Script decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| "In 1901, a 63-year-old woman" | Stunt on 24 Oct 1901, her 63rd birthday (born 24 Oct 1838); she told the press she was in her 40s | confirmed (multiple sources; one 1901 transcription says 43 = her own claim) | keep |
| "went over Niagara Falls ... inside a wooden barrel" | Horseshoe Falls, custom oak-and-iron barrel (~4.5 ft), cushions, anvil ballast | confirmed | keep |
| "she had a plan to get rich" | sought fame and money for her old age | confirmed | keep |
| "First, she tested her barrel with a cat. It came back alive." | a cat went over in the barrel two days before (some say 18 Oct); most accounts: survived (head cut / barely a scratch); Michigan Public: conflicting reports | disputed detail | soften: "By most accounts, it came back alive." |
| "strapped herself in, had the lid sealed" | harness + cushions inside; lid screwed down, air pumped in | confirmed | keep |
| "One hundred and sixty-seven feet." | 167 ft (51 m) = World Waterfall Database; 188 ft (57 m) crest-to-river = Niagara Parks; 158 ft = EBSCO | disputed figure | soften: "More than a hundred and sixty feet." (true for both main figures) |
| "rescuers finally opened the barrel, Annie was alive" | pulled to shore and the lid sawn/opened; alive | confirmed | keep |
| "relatively minor injuries" | a gash on her head, bruises | confirmed | keep |
| "barely made any money" | little income from lectures/photos; manager Frank M. Russell took the barrel; she spent savings on detectives; died poor in 1921 | confirmed | keep |

Dramatization (not documentary fact, shown as cartoon): the rivermen, Annie on the shore at the cat test, the rescue
blocking, the coin purse.
