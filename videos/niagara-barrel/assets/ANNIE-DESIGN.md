# Annie Edson Taylor — 2D character brief
Original stylized design inspired by 1901 era, not a photo trace. Older woman (63), compact figure ~3.5 head-heights, pale cream face, dark plum outlines, small dot eyes, brows able to convey worry/resolve, gray-brown hair in tidy bun, small dark hat, cream high-collar blouse, muted teal long skirt, brown ankle boots. Readable silhouette in vertical phone framing. Expressive poses: determined, anxious in barrel cutaway, dazed on rescue, deadpan at empty purse. Follow existing toon.ts/cast.ts design language; character is a camera-facing lit billboard with matching contact shadow. No extra text labels.
