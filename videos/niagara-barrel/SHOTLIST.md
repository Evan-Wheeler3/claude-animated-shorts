# Niagara Barrel — Direction / shot list
Vertical 9:16. ~38–42 sec at 30fps. Beat lengths flex to recorded voice. NO captions, titles, logo or lower thirds. Original cartoon characters in 3D outlined environment.

1. 0–4s HOOK: Very wide Horseshoe Falls, tiny barrel sweeps into frame, rapid controlled push toward lip, dramatic tilt down. On "wooden barrel", hard match cut to barrel close-up; Annie's eyes peeking from hatch in stylized cutaway (not a literal window).
2. 4–9s MOTIVE: 1901 riverside; Annie points to imagined pile of coins, hopeful grin; camera circles to reveal looming falls behind her. Short push-in on determined expression.
3. 9–14s CAT TEST: Assistants place cat into barrel, close it; fast visual montage of barrel bobbing in choppy water, rescuers open it, cat hops out indignant. No invented injuries.
4. 14–20s SEAL: Medium on Annie climbing into barrel, overhead of padding/straps and lid; close-up of hammer tapping last fastener; match the last tap to roar of water.
5. 20–29s PLUNGE: Tracking water-level shot, barrel spinning along current; extreme wide at brink to convey scale; camera follows from safe distance, then splash/mist fills frame. Don't show character outside barrel.
6. 29–34s RESCUE: River below; rescuers pull barrel to safety, lift lid; Annie pops up dazed but alive. Quick reaction close-up.
7. 34–42s TWIST: triumphant imaginary gold rains down; smash to empty coin purse and tiny pile of coins, Annie deadpan. Camera slow push, end immediately on last word.

Camera movement: motivated follows, whip pans on action, 2–3 detail inserts, scale contrast, no pointless constant orbiting. Freeze 1–2 frames for comic emphasis if useful. Splash effects must remain legible on mobile. Ambient roar, creaking wood, one sharp plunge impact, subdued comic payoff. Voice stays clearly dominant.
