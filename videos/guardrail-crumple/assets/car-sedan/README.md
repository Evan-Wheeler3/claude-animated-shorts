# Hold Up sedan (toon pass)

`Car_Sedan.glb` is copied unchanged from `videos/phil-bathroom/assets/props/Car_Sedan.glb` (commit `1e99cdc`, licence "Original for Hold Up" in the phil-bathroom asset manifest). It has real glazing, a full cabin, a driver seat anchor, steering wheel, separate front bumper and four wheel nodes.

`../sedan.ts` (`loadSedan(url)`) turns it into the guardrail toon look at load time. It drops the textures, gives every material a flat matte colour from the Codex palette (body paint `#f9673e`), makes the glass light and see-through on the NOINK layer, and makes the materials double-sided because the roof and bumper caps are single-sided. It also fixes the corners. The GLB's side sheets pinched inward past the lights and bumpers, so the bumper ends poked through the bodywork and the sheet ends read as loose flaps. `sedan.ts` folds each sheet's last 0.1 m tip back flush with the lights, adds a small end plate behind each bumper corner, narrows the bumpers to sit inside the sheets (×0.91) and tucks in the taillight lenses (×0.96). Before and after: `previews/sedan-corner-fix.png`. It also turns the car 180° so the nose faces +Z, scales it to 4.1 m long, puts the ground at Y=0, and adds pivots at the centres of the wheels and steering wheel, since the GLB has no node transforms. The driver sits at +X (left-hand drive).

Look check: `previews/sedan-toon-look.png` (Remotion composition `GuardrailCarLook`, frames 0–3).
