One man got rich in the Gold Rush selling shovels.

In 1849, thousands rushed to California hoping to strike it rich. Samuel Brannan stocked his store with the tools they'd need.

A gold pan that once cost twenty cents? Miners were paying eight dollars for one.

Supplies were scarce, and the miners kept buying.

One report estimated a miner's daily haul at sixteen dollars in gold. Brannan's store reportedly took in over two thousand dollars a day.

The miners went looking for a fortune. The man selling shovels just waited for them to bring it to him.
