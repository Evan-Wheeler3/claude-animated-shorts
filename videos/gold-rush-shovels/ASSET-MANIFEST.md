# Gold Rush Shovels asset manifest

All paths below are relative to `videos/gold-rush-shovels/`. The episode uses procedural Three.js groups and Canvas2D character cards. One world unit is one meter, Y is up, and the cast faces local +Z. Preview status is **owner review needed**.

| ID | Source and provenance | Scale, pivot and controls | Review still |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MINER | `assets/cast.ts` · original period costume, Bridge bean-head rig, face library and card shader | `makeCharacter("miner")`, boot-plane root, roughly 1.9 m with hat. `setPose` sets expression, facing and crouch. `faceCamera` sets yaw. `aimHand` targets a world point or a prop socket. | [Opening slap](previews/opening-slap.jpg), [pan grip](previews/pan-close-up.jpg) |
| BRANNAN | `assets/cast.ts` · original waistcoat, collar and bow tie on Bridge stout build | `makeCharacter("brannan")`, boot-plane root. Same hand controls with grip, pinch, open and index shapes. | [Counter trade](previews/counter-trade.jpg), [drawer release](previews/drawer-release.jpg) |
| CROWD | `assets/cast.ts` · original wardrobe palettes, Bridge body and expressions | Roles `red-cap`, `wide-hat`, `blue-cap`, each with a distinct build, complexion or headwear. Boot-plane root. | [Creek cast](previews/creek-cast.jpg), [shop overview](previews/shop-overview.jpg) |
| CREEK | `assets/sets.ts` · original gravel bar, rocks, tree and hill placement, Bridge painted bed and water | `makeCreek()`, bar surface at Y=0 and water at Y=-0.075. `barSurface` locates a flat staging area and `setFlow(seconds)` scrolls the water texture. | [Creek cast](previews/creek-cast.jpg) |
| SHOP | `assets/sets.ts` · original timber geometry, Bridge wood maps and two-tone shader | `makeShop()`. Floor Y=0, counter Y=0.97, back-wall threshold at Z=-2.2. `panRack` contains individually removable copies of the same pan. Includes shelves, crate and ledger. | [Shop overview](previews/shop-overview.jpg) |
| PAN | `assets/props.ts` · original lathed metal pan | `makePan()`. Base center pivot, ~0.45 m diameter and 0.077 m deep. Floor Y=0.012. `left` and `right` are diametric rim grips. Wet gravel and water-film dressing are in `qa/poses.ts`. | [Pan grip](previews/pan-close-up.jpg), [wet gravel](previews/nugget-in-gravel.jpg) |
| WORN SHOVEL | `assets/props.ts` · original scuffed steel blade and splintered wooden shaft | `makeShovel(true)` pivots on blade edge at root. Local +Y along the 1.19 m shaft, +Z at blade face. `setBroken(true)` swaps shaft geometry and exposes the independent `upper` pivot at Y=0.43. Keep `lower` planted. `lowerGrip` and `upperGrip` travel with the upper piece. | [Intact](previews/shovel-before.jpg), [split](previews/shovel-broken.jpg) |
| FRESH SHOVEL | `assets/props.ts` · shared blade profile, fresh wood and clean steel | `makeShovel()`. Same root, grips and `bladeTouch`. Lay the underside against the table with `rest` at table height, 0.031 m beneath the shaft axis in the proof orientation. | [Opening slap](previews/opening-slap.jpg), [counter trade](previews/counter-trade.jpg) |
| NUGGET | `assets/props.ts` · original faceted gold hull | `makeNugget()`. ~38 × 24 × 27 mm. Root is the flat base. `pinch` is at Y=0.015. Reparent a single instance from pan to hand to counter to tray during assembly. | [Wet gravel](previews/nugget-in-gravel.jpg), [drawer landing](previews/drawer-landed.jpg) |
| DRAWER | `assets/props.ts` and `assets/sets.ts` · original timber joinery | `makeShop().drawer`. Closed tray root at (0.67, 0.742, 0.097). `setOpen(0..1)` travels +Z up to 0.34 m. `grip` follows the ring and `landing` follows the tray floor, Y=0.014 locally. | [Release](previews/drawer-release.jpg), [landing](previews/drawer-landed.jpg) |

New code and procedural artwork are repository contributions under the root MIT license. The existing Bridge template supplies the rig, painted textures, card and material shaders, ink, lens blur and grade. The library also includes Niagara rock, purse, boat and barrel models. The creek uses scaled procedural pebbles and the shallow water setup for this episode. No downloaded meshes, image textures or external fonts are required.
