# Why Traffic Lights Are Red, Yellow And Green: script v1 (Fix-Fail Chain, for the script gate)

Narrator voice B. ~175 words (~55 s). Experiment (LEARNING-LOG.md): **opening only** - the most surprising fact, happening
on frame 1, then a second surprise by ~5 s. Everything after follows the safety-matches format that held 139 %.
Title options: "The First Traffic Light Exploded" · "Why Traffic Lights Are Red, Yellow And Green" · "Why Yellow Lights Exist"

L01 The world's first traffic light exploded, and badly hurt the policeman running it.
L02 After that, Britain went sixty years without another one.
L03 So police stood in the middle of busy streets, directing traffic by hand, for hours, even in the rain.
L04 Then a policeman in Salt Lake City built an electric light. Red and green, because trains and ships already used them. But drivers just ignored it.
L05 Worse, it jumped straight from green to red. Fast drivers had no time to stop.
L06 Some towns tried leaving the green on for a few seconds after the red came on. Both at once!
L07 Finally, a policeman in Detroit added a third light in the middle. Yellow, meaning: the light is about to change.
L08 Then timers took over, and New York moved more than five thousand traffic cops to other jobs.
L09 So every yellow light is a warning. *(pause, ~0.5 s)* Which is more than that first policeman ever got.
